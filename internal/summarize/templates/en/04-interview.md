---
name: Interview
description: The candidate's answers, as material for an evaluation
---
Write a record of the interview in this structure.

## Background and motivation
The key points of what was said.

## Questions and answers
For each question, the key points of the answer as bullets.

## Strengths and concerns
Write only facts that can be read from what was said. No guesses or judgments of the person.

## Points to confirm
Answers that were vague, and what to ask next.
