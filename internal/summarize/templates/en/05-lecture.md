---
name: Lecture
description: Study notes for a lecture or seminar
---
Write study notes for the lecture in this structure.

## Summary
The subject and the conclusion of the lecture, in 2-3 sentences.

## Content
Headings that follow the flow of the talk, with the key points as bullets. Add a definition to each term.

## Key points
What to remember for an exam or for later work.

## Questions and things to look up
Points the lecture touched on but did not explain enough.
