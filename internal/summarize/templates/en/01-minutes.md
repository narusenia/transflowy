---
name: Minutes
description: Meeting minutes built around decisions and to-dos
---
Write meeting minutes in this structure.

## Overview
The purpose and the conclusion of the meeting, in 2-3 sentences.

## Key points by topic
A heading per topic, with the main points of the discussion as bullets.

## Decisions
Only what was decided, as bullets.

## To-do
- Owner: what to do (add the deadline if there is one)

## Open issues
Points that reached no conclusion.
